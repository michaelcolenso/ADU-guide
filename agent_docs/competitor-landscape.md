# Competitor Landscape

Read this when writing marketing copy, positioning statements, or when you need to understand what makes this product different.

## Why There's No Real Competitor

Every existing ADU product has at least one of these fatal weaknesses: outdated (pre-dates 2024–2025 legislation), geographically locked (California or Portland only), too thin (80–100 pages), professionally oriented (for realtors, not homeowners), or structured as marketing for a construction business. No single product covers the full ADU lifecycle with current information across multiple states.

## Key Competitors

**Kol Peterson / Backdoor Revolution**
- Book: ~$25, published 2018. Thoughtful and thorough but pre-dates CA's 2020 legislation, most state ADU laws, and uses grainy B&W photos. Heavily Portland/Oregon-focused.
- Online course: $165, 5 hours, 20 classes. Most comprehensive educational product that exists, but Portland-centric and not substantially updated.
- Consultation: $250 for 1.5–2 hours on-site in Portland.
- Vulnerability: Age and geographic scope. Our guide covers 10x the regulatory territory with 2025–2026 data.

**ADU Geeks Masterclass**
- Price: $150, launched 2025. San Diego/California-focused. Tied to a design-build company (conflict of interest). No public reviews yet.
- Vulnerability: Regional lock-in and business affiliation. We're independent.

**Modern ADU Plans**
- Starter pack: $49.99. Individual tools: $14.99–$29.99. Primarily an architecture firm monetizing floor plans.
- Vulnerability: Sells individual tools, not a comprehensive guide. No lifecycle coverage.

**Udemy**
- One ADU-specific course, by a lender, California-focused. Generic construction PM courses get 20,000–40,000+ students.
- Vulnerability: Single perspective (lender), single state.

**Amazon Books**
- 6–9 ADU books total. Best one is Peterson (2018). Newer entries draw 1-star reviews for being thin and repetitive. No book is both comprehensive and current with 2024–2025 legislation.

**Digital Marketplaces**
- Gumroad: Zero ADU guides.
- Etsy: Zero ADU-specific templates (2,000+ generic construction budget spreadsheets at $5–$25).
- This is our primary opportunity: first mover on the platform with the most natural fit.

## Our Differentiation (Use in Marketing)

"Written by a construction project manager, not a contractor selling services." Independent perspective from the owner's side of the table, covering 26+ targeted state references with 2025–2026 legislative tracking, updated regularly (digital advantage over print), and priced at a fraction of a single consultation hour.

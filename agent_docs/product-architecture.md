## Tier Positioning

**Foundation ($97):** "The essential roadmap." For exploration-phase homeowners who need comprehensive understanding before committing. Expected ~30% of sales.

**Builder ($197):** "The project manager's toolkit." For committed homeowners who need professional-grade tools. **This is the value sweet spot — design everything to make this tier feel like a steal. Expected ~45% of sales.**

**Complete ($297):** "The full construction management system." Replaces paid consultations. Expected ~20% of sales, plus ~5% upgrades from lower tiers.

**Blended AOV target: ~$167.**

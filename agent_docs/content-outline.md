# Content Outline — Complete Chapter Reference

Read this document before writing any chapter. It defines the scope, key topics, and deliverables for each chapter. The overall guide is structured around the ADU lifecycle — the actual sequence of decisions a homeowner faces from first consideration through tenant move-in.

---

## Part I: Should You Build an ADU?

### Chapter 1 — The ADU Landscape in 2025–2026
**Scope:** Set context and manage expectations. What an ADU is, the four types (detached, attached, garage conversion, JADU), market snapshot with cost ranges by type and region, the legislative shift across at least 18 states with enacted ADU laws as of September 15, 2025 (plus broader partial reforms), and who this guide is for.
**Length:** 15–20 pages.
**Deliverables:** None (context-setting chapter).

### Chapter 2 — Feasibility Assessment: Can Your Property Support an ADU?
**Scope:** Zoning verification (with annotated ordinance examples), lot coverage/setback/height constraints, utility infrastructure (sewer, electrical, water), HOA and CC&R restrictions (and when state law overrides them), environmental and historic overlays, the 30-minute DIY site assessment.
**Deliverable:** Property Feasibility Scorecard (fillable PDF) — all tiers.

### Chapter 3 — The Financial Case: ROI, Rental Income, and Property Value
**Scope:** Rental income projections by market, property value impact (the 9.34% growth premium data), multigenerational value quantification, tax implications (depreciation, rental income, capital gains), break-even analysis framework.
**Deliverable:** ROI & Rental Income Calculator (Excel) — Complete tier only.

---

## Part II: Design and Planning

### Chapter 4 — ADU Design Fundamentals
**Scope:** Choosing ADU type based on constraints/budget/use case, size optimization (600–800 sq ft sweet spot), universal design for aging-in-place, pre-approved vs custom plans tradeoffs, working with architects (scope, fees, expectations), common design mistakes that blow budgets.
**Deliverable:** Design Decision Matrix (comparing ADU types across 12 criteria) — all tiers.

### Chapter 5 — Your State's ADU Rules: A Regulatory Reference
**Scope:** Consistent framework applied to each state. State-by-state coverage using `regulations/_template.md` format: enabling legislation, key provisions, local override rules, city-level highlights for top ADU markets, the "regulation change tracker" methodology.
**Tier coverage:** Foundation = CA, WA, OR, MA, CO. Builder adds TX, FL, AZ, HI, VT, MN, MT, UT, CT, ME. Complete = all targeted 26+ state references.
**Deliverable:** The state reference sections themselves are the deliverable.

---

## Part III: Financing Your ADU

### Chapter 6 — Financing Options Compared
**Scope:** HELOCs and home equity loans, construction loans (draw schedule process), Fannie Mae HomeStyle, FHA 203(k), state/local programs (CalHFA $40K grant, Massachusetts programs), cash-out refinance, creative financing (ADU-specific lenders, shared equity, family loans), side-by-side comparison matrix.
**Deliverable:** Financing Comparison Matrix (Excel) — Foundation gets summary, Builder/Complete get interactive version with scenario modeling.

---

## Part IV: Building Your ADU

### Chapter 7 — Assembling Your Team: Contractor Selection
**Scope:** Contractor landscape (GCs, design-build, prefab, owner-builder), 15-question vetting interview with scoring rubric, reading and comparing bids (line-item breakdown method), 12 red flags, contract essentials (fixed-price vs cost-plus, payment schedules, change orders), insurance verification.
**Deliverables (Builder & Complete):** Contractor Vetting Scorecard, Bid Comparison Template, Contract Review Checklist.

### Chapter 8 — The Permit Process Demystified
**Scope:** Typical permit workflow (pre-application through approval), required documents (site plans, structural engineering, Title 24, soils), timeline expectations by jurisdiction, communicating with building departments, common rejection reasons, pre-approved plans shortcut.
**Deliverable:** Permit Process Checklists — Builder (5 cities: LA, SF, Seattle, Portland, Denver), Complete (25 cities).

### Chapter 9 — Construction Management for Homeowners
**Scope:** Construction timeline by phase, homeowner PM dashboard (weekly tracking), draw schedule management, change order evaluation and negotiation, 8 critical inspection quality checkpoints, communication protocols (weekly meetings, photo docs, decision logs), dispute resolution and contractor replacement.
**Deliverable (Builder & Complete):** PM Timeline Template (editable Gantt).

### Chapter 10 — Budgeting and Cost Control
**Scope:** True cost breakdown (hard costs, soft costs, contingency, hidden costs), regional benchmarks with verification data sources, contingency framework, value engineering (where to save without sacrificing quality), cost creep prevention (weekly tracking methodology).
**Deliverable:** Budget Calculator — Foundation (basic), Builder/Complete (advanced with regional benchmarks).

## Part V: After Construction

### Chapter 11 — Inspections, Certificate of Occupancy, and Closeout
**Scope:** Final inspection preparation (pre-inspection walkthrough), common failures and remedies, CO meaning and post-CO steps, punch list management, warranty documentation, as-built drawings and archiving.
**Deliverable (Complete):** Pre-Inspection Walkthrough Checklist (50+ points).

### Chapter 12 — Monetizing Your ADU
**Scope:** Long-term rental (screening, leases, property management basics), short-term rental (regulations, setup, optimization), multigenerational use (legal/financial), ADU as condo (CA AB 1033), rental ADU insurance, annual financial review checklist.
**Deliverable (Complete):** Tenant Screening & Lease Template Kit.

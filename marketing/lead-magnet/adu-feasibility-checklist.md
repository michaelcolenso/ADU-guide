# ADU Feasibility Checklist

Use this checklist before you hire a designer or request contractor bids. If you can confidently check at least 80% of the items below, your project is usually ready for pre-design and early pricing.

## 1) Confirm Your Legal Baseline

- [ ] I confirmed my zoning district and whether ADUs are allowed by right, ministerially, or with conditions.
- [ ] I confirmed whether my parcel has any overlay rules (coastal, wildfire, floodplain, historic, hillside, or environmental).
- [ ] I confirmed the number of ADUs allowed on my lot and whether attached, detached, and conversion ADUs are all permitted.
- [ ] I confirmed whether owner-occupancy is required for my city or county.
- [ ] I verified if short-term rental use is restricted for ADUs.

## 2) Confirm Basic Site Fit

- [ ] I measured likely ADU placement options and checked setbacks, height, and lot coverage.
- [ ] I identified where an ADU entrance could go without privacy conflicts.
- [ ] I identified whether I need to preserve on-site parking or can remove/relocate existing parking.
- [ ] I confirmed I have practical construction access for excavation, framing, and inspections.
- [ ] I flagged slope, retaining walls, large trees, and drainage constraints.

## 3) Confirm Utility Reality

- [ ] I understand current electrical service size (for example, 100A vs 200A) and whether a panel upgrade is likely.
- [ ] I understand likely sewer and water connection path and whether capacity upgrades may be required.
- [ ] I confirmed whether impact fees or utility connection fees apply to my target ADU size.
- [ ] I identified utility trenching path length and surface restoration risk (driveway, hardscape, mature landscaping).

## 4) Confirm Governance and Restrictions

- [ ] I reviewed CC&Rs and HOA rules for design controls, rental limits, and architecture review timelines.
- [ ] I confirmed whether state law limits local or HOA restrictions in my specific jurisdiction.
- [ ] I verified there are no deed restrictions or easements that block the intended ADU location.

## 5) Confirm Financial Readiness

- [ ] I built a preliminary budget with hard costs, soft costs, and contingency.
- [ ] I included a contingency reserve of at least 10-15%.
- [ ] I tested a realistic rent assumption and a conservative rent assumption.
- [ ] I estimated my break-even timeline under both assumptions.
- [ ] I confirmed how I will finance predevelopment costs before construction starts.

## 6) Decision Gate

If you missed mostly legal or site-fit items, pause and resolve feasibility first. If you missed mostly finance items, proceed only after modeling a conservative scenario. If you missed utility items, expect budget and timeline surprises unless you complete utility due diligence before final design.

## Next Step (30 Minutes)

Walk your property with a tape measure, your parcel map, and this checklist. Mark one preferred ADU location and one backup location. Then call your planning counter with your APN and ask for a quick zoning and standards confirmation before spending on plans.
